Method

Transparent so you can check each number.

  • Flow: source MW × (1 − routine leakage) ÷ LHV (120 MJ/kg) gives kg/h. Net annual delivery = kg/h × 8,760 h × utilisation.
  • Velocity and friction: inlet density from P, Z, T. Average velocity uses mean pressure. Friction factor from Colebrook (iterative).
  • Hydraulic capacity: flow at which the Darcy pressure drop over one section equals inlet minus outlet pressure.
  • Compression: one station per spacing interval, each raising pressure from outlet back to inlet (ratio P_in/P_out). Isentropic power, cp = 14.3 kJ/kg·K, k = 1.41, divided by compressor efficiency.
  • Cost: (pipeline + compressor CAPEX) × CRF + fixed OPEX + compressor electricity, divided by net annual kg.
  • Leakage: routine loss only, at the stated % per 1000 km. External accidental release is not modelled.
Research guardrail: dedicated and repurposed pipelines are not assumed equivalent. This page models dedicated H₂ only. Accidental buried-pipeline leaks and their consequences are outside the routine-loss model.
