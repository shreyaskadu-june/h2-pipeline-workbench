Method and limits

Each step is stated so it can be checked against the numbers above.

  • Flow. Source MW × (1 − routine leakage) ÷ LHV (119.96 MJ/kg) gives kg/h. Net annual delivery = kg/h × 8,760 h × utilisation.
  • Velocity and friction. Inlet density from P, Z and T. Average velocity uses mean pressure. Friction factor from Colebrook, solved iteratively.
  • Hydraulic capacity. The flow at which the Darcy pressure drop over one section equals (inlet − outlet pressure) × efficiency.
  • Compression. One station per spacing interval, each raising pressure from outlet back to inlet (ratio Pin/Pout). Isentropic power with cp = 14.45 kJ/kg·K and k = 1.417, divided by compressor efficiency.
  • Cost. (Pipeline + compressor CAPEX) × CRF + fixed OPEX + compressor electricity, divided by net annual kg.
  • Leakage. Routine loss only, at the stated % per 1000 km. External accidental release is not modelled.
  • Interpretations. The pipeline efficiency factor multiplies the allowable pressure drop when computing hydraulic capacity. The 20 m/s (amber) and 30 m/s (red) velocity bands are screening choices, not a published standard. Discount rate is 7% nominal, an investor-rate assumption. For reference, Ofgem's final RIIO-3 gas WACC is 5.18% (semi-nominal, regulated network, low risk); no published UK hydrogen-pipeline investor hurdle rate was found, so 7% is a judgement to be replaced with a project's own rate. Station spacing, leakage, OPEX and lifetime are still inherited defaults and are not independently sourced.
  • Scope. Dedicated H₂ pipelines only. Repurposed gas pipelines are not assumed equivalent and are not modelled here.
  • Defaults. Electricity is sourced (DESNZ, Q4 2025, manufacturing). Pipeline CAPEX is sourced (HyNet/Cadent, restated to 2026 and scaled to DN800); compressor and threshold values are placeholders in GBP. Compression is sized from the imposed pressure ratio, not from the hydraulic drop, so compressor power is a design choice; the hydraulic drop over one section is only about 1 bar at these flows.
